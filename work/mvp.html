ESS LGS — Automated Admissions & HR

Workflow Automation

The Context

Processing thousands of student admission applications and teacher recruitment cycles manually created severe paper bottlenecks and delays.

ESS LGS needed a secure, end-to-end automated portal where prospective students apply online and HR teams publish and manage faculty openings.

Project year - 2025

The Solution

We developed a full-stack automation portal using Next.js, Prisma, Tailwind CSS, and NextAuth with granular role-based authorization.

The platform processed 2,000+ admissions with 95% workflow automation and an 80% reduction in administrative turnaround time.
